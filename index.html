Who am I

I build things that matter, and I bring people along to build them.

I am a second year computer science student who simply loves to build. Give me an idea, a rough sketch or a problem nobody has solved well, and I will happily lose track of time turning it into something real. Ambition got me started. Purpose is what keeps me going.

I have led as Head Girl, spoken to full rooms, run PR for many fests and hackathons, and, as a freelancer, helped companies grow. The lesson never changes: great work needs clear communication, honest collaboration and people who trust one another. I am at my best when a team achieves something none of us could have done alone.

I am endlessly amused by parallel realities, and the question of what else could be true. And somewhere behind all of it, music is always playing. It has shaped how I think, write and lead.
